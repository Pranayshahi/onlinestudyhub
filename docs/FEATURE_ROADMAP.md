# 🚀 OnlineStudyHub — Competitive EdTech Feature Roadmap

A strategic roadmap of next-generation features to outperform leading online study platforms (**PhysicsWallah, Unacademy, Vedantu, Khan Academy, Allen Digital, and Doubtnut**).

---

## 🏆 Summary of Platform Positioning

OnlineStudyHub currently offers **100% free ICSE & CBSE Class 6–12, JEE & NEET topic notes**, 458+ chapters, 1,887+ solved past board Q&As, AI NotebookLM audio podcasts, 1-on-1 teacher bookings, NTA mock tests, and a student doubt community feed.

To achieve total market leadership and maximize student retention, the following high-yield feature modules are scheduled for upcoming development phases.

---

## 1. 📷 Snap & Solve AI Camera Doubt Solver
- **Concept**: Allow students to snap a photo of any handwritten or textbook problem on mobile/tablet.
- **Tech Stack**: OCR (Tesseract.js / Google Vision API) + Gemini 1.5 Flash / Sarvam AI.
- **Value Proposition**: Instant step-by-step solution rendering with LaTeX mathematical formatting and related video lecture suggestions.

---

## 2. 🔥 Gamified Student Study Streaks, XP & Leaderboards
- **Concept**: Drive daily student engagement through study streaks, experience points (XP), and weekly national/state leaderboards.
- **Features**:
  - **Daily Streaks**: Flame icon streak counter for visiting and completing at least 1 topic daily.
  - **XP & Badges**: Earn badges like *"Calculus Wizard"*, *"Organic Synthesizer"*, *"NEET Bio Master"*.
  - **Weekly Leaderboard**: Rank students based on quiz accuracy, community answers, and study time.

---

## 3. 🎯 AI Adaptive Learning Path & Weak Area Diagnostic Engine
- **Concept**: An intelligent diagnostic engine that analyzes student mock test failures and topic quiz mistakes.
- **Features**:
  - Automatically identifies weak chapters (e.g., *"Rotational Dynamics accuracy < 40%"*).
  - Generates a customized daily revision queue with targeted flashcards and recommended practice questions.

---

## 4. 🧪 Interactive 3D Virtual Science Labs & PhET Simulations
- **Concept**: Embedded 3D interactive physics and chemistry lab simulations directly inside topic pages.
- **Features**:
  - **Physics Lab**: Ray optics lens simulator, projectile launcher, simple pendulum frequency adjuster.
  - **Chemistry Lab**: Virtual titration, 3D molecular structure viewer (Three.js / PDB viewer).

---

## 5. 📲 Parent WhatsApp Progress Reports & Attendance Portal
- **Concept**: Keep parents informed and involved without friction.
- **Features**:
  - **Automated WhatsApp Reports**: Send monthly PDF summaries of quiz scores, study hours, and teacher attendance directly via WhatsApp API (Twilio / Meta Graph API).
  - **Parent Dashboard**: Real-time view of upcoming booked sessions, completed chapters, and test analytics.

---

## 6. 🎙️ Live WebRTC Classroom & "Raise Hand" Audio Doubt Session
- **Concept**: Upgrade teacher booking sessions with an integrated in-app live classroom interface.
- **Features**:
  - Built-in WebRTC video/audio streaming with collaborative whiteboard (Excalidraw integration).
  - **Raise Hand Feature**: Students can raise their hand to unmute and ask verbal doubts directly to the mentor.
  - Real-time in-class MCQ polls with instant result charts.

---

## 7. 📶 Progressive Web App (PWA) & Offline Notes Mode
- **Concept**: Seamless learning for students in low-connectivity or rural areas.
- **Features**:
  - **Offline Caching**: Service worker caching for all chapter notes, formula cheat sheets, and flashcards.
  - **Downloadable Audio**: One-click download for NotebookLM audio podcast summaries.

---

## 8. ⏱️ Virtual Pomodoro Group Study Rooms & Co-Working Hub
- **Concept**: Social study environment for peer motivation during long study hours.
- **Features**:
  - Silent 25min / 5min study rooms with embedded Lo-Fi study beats and shared goal timers.
  - Live study counters showing active study partners across India.
